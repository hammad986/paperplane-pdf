## Scope and status meaning

The production service was not changed, as requested. `FIXED` means the local branch change passed its local regression or observed UI check. It does not mean the change is deployed. `PARTIAL` means a change or some evidence exists, but a required acceptance check, provider, or post-deployment check is missing. `NOT TESTED` means no relevant post-change check was performed.

The HOLD is required because the 69-page Summarize/Brief and Translate outputs could not be generated without a configured AI provider; the supplied Drive link resolves to a different, one-page PDF; several acceptance checks require the owner’s deployed build; and the mandatory all-41-tools content audit was not completed twice.
