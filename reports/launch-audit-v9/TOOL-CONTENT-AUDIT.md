## Limits

These runs exercise `server.process`, the same local worker function called by queued jobs, and open every produced artifact. They do not replace UI/API acceptance, the exact owner file, production credentials, the real 69-page provider cases, or owner-host/volume/proxy verification. A tool that failed because an upstream credential or runtime dependency is absent is not a product pass.
